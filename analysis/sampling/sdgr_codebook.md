# SDGR Annex I area codebook

Codes for the `annotation` column in `manual_annotation_sample.csv`. Definitions are
reproduced verbatim from `analysis/sdg/sdg_categorize_full.py` (the zero-shot classifier's prompt) so the manual annotation guide always matches
what the automated classifier is actually instructed to do.

| Code | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| A | Travel within the EU | Travel: identity and travel documents (ID card, passport, visa); rights of passengers travelling by plane, train, ship, or bus, and of people buying travel packages; assistance for travellers with reduced mobility; rules on transporting animals, plants, alcohol, tobacco, and other goods; rules on calls, electronic messages, and data while travelling. |
| B | Work and retirement | Work and retirement: seeking and taking up employment; recognition of qualifications for employment; taxation related to work; liability and mandatory insurance linked to employment; terms and conditions of employment (working hours, paid leave, holidays, overtime, health checks, termination, dismissal, redundancy); equal treatment and non-discrimination at work, equal pay; workplace health and safety obligations; social security rights and obligations, including pensions. |
| C | Vehicles | Vehicles: registering a motor vehicle; acquiring and renewing a driving licence; mandatory motor vehicle insurance; buying and selling a motor vehicle; traffic rules and requirements for drivers, including use of road infrastructure (vignette, tolls, emission stickers). |
| D | Residence in another EU country | Residence: moving home and registering residence; purchasing, selling, owning, or using property (including taxation and secondary residences); participating in municipal and European elections; residence cards and registration requirements; naturalisation and citizenship; rules applicable in the case of death, including repatriation of remains. |
| E | Education or traineeship | Education or traineeship: the education system (early childhood, primary, secondary, higher, and adult education); volunteering; traineeships; research as part of an education programme. |
| F | Healthcare | Healthcare: getting medical treatment; buying prescribed pharmaceuticals; health insurance rules, including the European Health Insurance Card; access to public preventive healthcare; emergency numbers (112, 116); moving to a residential care home. |
| G | Citizens and family rights | Citizens' and family rights: birth, custody, parental responsibility, surrogacy, adoption, child maintenance; marriage, civil and registered partnerships, separation, divorce, marital property, cohabitants' rights, including same-sex couples; gender recognition; succession and inheritance, including related tax; parental child abduction. |
| H | Consumer rights | Consumer rights: buying goods, digital content, or services (including financial services), online or in person; holding a bank account; connection to utilities (gas, electricity, water, waste disposal, telecoms, internet); payments and credit transfers; consumer rights, guarantees, dispute resolution, and compensation; safety of consumer products; renting a motor vehicle. |
| I | Protection of personal data | Protection of personal data: exercising data subjects' rights regarding the protection of personal data. |
| J | Starting, running, closing a business | Starting, running, and closing a business: registering, changing the legal form of, or closing a business; intellectual property (patents, trademarks, designs, licences); fair and transparent commercial practices; online payment facilities for selling goods/services; contract law and late-payment rules; insolvency and liquidation; credit insurance; mergers and selling a business; civil liability of company directors; processing of personal data by businesses. |
| K | Employees (employer-side) | Employees (employer-side): terms of employment set by law (working hours, leave, overtime, health checks, termination, dismissals, redundancies); social security obligations (registering as an employer, registering employees, notifying end of contract, paying contributions, pensions); employing workers, posting of workers, residency requirements for workers; equal treatment and equal pay; staff representation. |
| L | Taxes | Taxes: VAT (rules, rates, exemptions, registration, payment, refunds); excise duties (rules, rates, exemptions, registration, payment, refunds); customs duties and import taxes; customs procedures for imports and exports; other taxes (payment, rates, tax returns). |
| M | Goods | Goods: obtaining CE marking; product rules and requirements; standards, technical specifications, and product certification; mutual recognition of products; classification, labelling, and packaging of hazardous chemicals; distance and off-premises selling obligations; defective products, guarantees, and redress; certification and labels (EMAS, energy labels, Eco-design, EU Eco-label); recycling and waste management. |
| N | Services and professional qualifications | Services (business): acquiring licences, authorisations, or permits to start and operate a business; notifying authorities of business activities; recognition of professional qualifications, including vocational education and training. |
| O | Funding a business | Funding a business: access to finance and funding programmes and grants; initiatives for entrepreneurs (exchanges, mentoring programmes, and similar). |
| P | Public contracts | Public contracts: participating in public tenders (rules and procedures); submitting bids in response to public calls for tender; reporting irregularities in the tender process. |
| Q | Health and safety at work | Health and safety at work: health and safety obligations for different types of activity, including risk prevention, information, and training. |
| UNCLEAR | — | the page IS citizen-facing government content but does not clearly fit any single A-Q area, spans multiple unrelated areas with no dominant one, or has too little text to judge. |
| OUT_OF_SCOPE | — | the page is NOT citizen-facing rights/procedures/services content (e.g. news, press releases, organisation or contact directories, internal or institutional government documents, recreational content). |
